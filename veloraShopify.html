Velora — Shopify Storefront

Figma → Shopify (Dawn). Custom hero, luxury typography, responsive layout, and local webfonts.

Figma → Shopify Custom Dawn Section Local Webfonts Responsive Grid Perf-friendly

Velora is a luxury e-commerce concept built directly from a Figma layout and implemented on Shopify. I customized the Dawn theme with a pixel-accurate hero, Adamina & Poppins type system, and tuned spacing across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Why Shopify

  • Fast to launch with reliable checkout & built-in payments.
  • Theme editor lets non-devs manage sections, content, and colors.
  • Apps & integrations for shipping, reviews, analytics, CRM.
  • Hosting & security handled — no server maintenance.

Best for

  • Boutiques, lifestyle & beauty, accessories, DTC brands.
  • Catalogs up to a few hundred SKUs.
  • Teams that want design precision with simple daily ops.

Stack: Shopify (Liquid/CSS), Dawn, local Google fonts via Shopify CDN, accessible headings, CLS-safe font loading.
