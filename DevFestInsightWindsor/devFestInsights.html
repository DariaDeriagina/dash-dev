How to get the most out of DevFest (quick plan)

  • Go to wedevfest.com → open the Sessions section. Pick 2–3 talks you don’t want to miss (Cloud, AI, Full-Stack, Cybersecurity, Entrepreneurship).
  • Open each session → check the speaker. If they’re a Googler or local leader, add them on LinkedIn with a note: “Hi, I’m attending DevFest Windsor. Excited for your talk on …”
  • Bring your CV / portfolio link. After a session, introduce yourself in one sentence: “I’m a UX/UI & Web dev focused on clean, accessible interfaces.”
  • Check the sponsors wall below — you might meet your future employer, client, or startup partner.
  • Charge your phone/laptop, wear something comfy, and stay for hallway conversations — that’s where magic happens.
