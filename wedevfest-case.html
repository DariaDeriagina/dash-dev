Context & Goals

Wedevfest.com is the home for DevFest Windsor 2025. My task was to design and code a system that would serve multiple audiences: attendees, speakers, sponsors, and students joining the AI competition.

  • Clear information architecture: Home, About, Speakers, Schedule, AI Competition.
  • Reusable speaker cards and modal-based bio detail view.
  • Fast performance with minimal JavaScript and strong accessibility support.
  • Reusable design system so future organizers can maintain the site.

Highlights

  • Scales to 30+ speakers via a template system.
  • AI Competition minisite acts as its own campaign hub.
  • Sticky mobile schedule tabs simplify navigation.

Information Architecture & Flows

I mapped three key journeys: Discover → Explore sessions → Register, Discover → Speaker bios, and AI Competition → Submit.
